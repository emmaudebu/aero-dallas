# AERO DALLAS — Launch checklist

Everything below must be confirmed by the business owner before the website goes live. Nothing on this list is shown on the public site until it has been entered: empty contact fields simply do not render.

## Company details (Appearance → Customize → AERO DALLAS — Company Details)

- [ ] Registered business address
- [ ] Official business email address
- [ ] Countries or regions served
- [ ] Business hours
- [ ] Quote notification email (where website quote requests are sent; defaults to the site admin email)

## Business terms

- [ ] Payment methods and payment terms
- [ ] Actual warranty terms
- [ ] Whether services are onsite, in-house, or through third-party partners
- [ ] Company registration details
- [ ] Approved cancellation and refund conditions

## Website

- [ ] Final website URL (update Settings → General → WordPress Address and Site Address when moving to the live domain)
- [ ] SMTP or transactional email configured so quote notifications are delivered reliably (see README → Email delivery)
- [ ] Send a test quote request on the live site and confirm the email arrives
- [ ] Replace or approve the stock photographs (Appearance → Customize → Photos, and featured images under Site Content → Services)
- [ ] Settings → Reading: "Discourage search engines" is **off**
- [ ] Choose the default appearance (light or dark) in Customize → Appearance
- [ ] Optional sections stay hidden until real content exists: only add **confirmed** statistics, **approved** client testimonials, and enable "Latest Insights" once articles are published

## Service page copy

- [ ] Review and approve the detailed write-ups on the four service pages. They were drafted from the content brief and policies for the website and have not yet been confirmed by the business. Check in particular the "What's Included" lists and "What to include in your request" guidance for each service.

## Legal review

- [ ] Legal review of all seven policies (General Business; Quotation & Payment; Service & Delivery; Cancellation & Refund; Warranty & After-Sales; Health, Safety & Quality; Privacy)
- [ ] The Privacy Policy must reflect actual website data practices and applicable privacy laws. In particular it should describe what the quote form collects (name, company, email, phone, service, equipment type, project description and any uploaded file), that submissions are stored in WordPress and emailed to the business, how long they are kept, and who to contact about them.
- [ ] The policy text was supplied as a draft pending company and legal review. That draft status is not shown on the website, so the review above must happen before launch.
